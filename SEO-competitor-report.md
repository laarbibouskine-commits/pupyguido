## 3. Where PuppyGuido is weaker (gaps)

1. Length: on competitive topics (potty schedule, crate, crying) competitors write 2,000-4,000 words; we are at 1,000-1,850.
2. Age-by-age sections (8 weeks, 10-12 weeks, 3-4 months, 4-6 months): we do not cover these headings, and they are the long-tail queries people search.
3. First week day-by-day plan: only as a short list.
4. Named author / reviewer: Google (and readers) like a person or vet-reviewed note. We only show "PuppyGuido".
5. FAQ schema: our FAQ text exists but should be marked up so we can win "People also ask".
6. Printable: top page offers a "printable by age" schedule; we have a free guide but no article-level printable.
7. Top answer box: no short "quick answer" box at the very top for featured snippets.
8. Internal links: few links between our own articles (topic clusters).

## 5. Recommended article template (apply to all new and old articles)

1. Title with primary keyword + year-free promise (max 60 chars), e.g. "Puppy Potty Training Schedule by Age (Printable)".
2. "Quick answer" box (40-60 words) directly under the title.
3. Table of contents with anchor links.
4. Length target: 1,800-2,500 words for competitive topics; 1,200-1,500 for long-tail questions.
5. Age-stage H2/H3 blocks where relevant: 8-10 weeks, 10-12 weeks, 3-4 months, 4-6 months.
6. One table (by age / by hour) and one numbered step list per article.
7. Mistakes section + "When to ask a vet or trainer" section (trust).
8. FAQ with 5-6 real questions (use "people also ask" phrasing) + FAQPage schema.
9. Author box: "Written by the PuppyGuido editorial team" plus "General information, not veterinary advice" and a "Reviewed on <date>" line (do not claim a vet reviewed it unless true).
10. 3-5 internal links to related articles, Amazon gear box (2 picks), free guide form.
11. Printable PDF checklist per cluster (potty, crate, first week) as a lead magnet.
