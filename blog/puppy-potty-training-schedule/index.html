Puppy Potty Training Schedule: A Simple Daily Routine That Works

Potty Training October 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Potty training is mostly about management and consistency. Puppies do not understand "rules" about where to go. They learn from routine, supervision and rewards.

The basics

  • Pick one spot. Take your puppy to the same area each time.
  • Go out often. Young puppies need frequent trips, often every 1 to 2 hours during the day.
  • Use a cue word. Say it calmly while your puppy sniffs and goes.
  • Reward right after. Praise and a treat the moment they finish, not when you are back inside.

When to take your puppy out

  1. First thing in the morning.
  2. After meals (usually within a short time after eating).
  3. After waking from naps.
  4. After play sessions.
  5. Before bed, and during the night if needed for very young puppies.

A sample day

TimeWhat to do
Wake upStraight outside to the potty spot, reward
BreakfastMeal, then potty break shortly after
Mid-morningPlay, short training, potty break, nap
MiddayMeal, potty, supervised time
AfternoonNap, potty on waking, play
EveningMeal, potty, calm time
BedtimeLast potty break, then sleep

This is a general example. Adjust to your puppy's age and your vet's advice.

Learn the signals

Sniffing the floor, circling, whining, suddenly wandering off or heading to the door can all mean "I need to go". When you see them, calmly take your puppy out.

What to do about accidents

  • Do not scold or rub the puppy's nose in it. It teaches fear, not toileting.
  • Clean with an enzymatic cleaner so the smell does not draw them back.
  • Ask yourself what you missed: a long gap, a missed signal, too much freedom.

Track progress

A simple log of meals, naps and potty breaks quickly shows your puppy's natural rhythm. The PupyGuido workbook includes trackers for exactly this.
