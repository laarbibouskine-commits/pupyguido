Training system

Simple. Gentle. Effective.

PupyGuido is built on positive reinforcement. No punishment, no scolding, just clear habits you and your puppy can build together.

Pause

Stay calm and stop the play for a moment when biting starts.

Redirect

Offer an appropriate chew toy instead of hands or furniture.

Reward

Praise and treat the moment your puppy makes the good choice.

Short sessions

A few minutes at a time keeps training fun and fits a puppy's attention span.

Reward good behavior

Puppies repeat what pays off. Catch the good moments and celebrate them.

Consistency

Same cues, same routine, same rules for the whole household.

No punishment-based advice. Ever. Patience and kindness build trust.
