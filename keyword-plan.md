## Rules for every article (SEO + quality)
- One clear question per article; answer it in the first 2 paragraphs.
- Natural headings only, never raw search phrases; no bold keyword stuffing.
- Link to 2-3 related articles + the free guide; link the ebook where the topic is emotional or schedule related.
- Health/safety topics: factual, no diagnosis, tell readers to contact their vet.
- Add a different photo and unique alt text for each article.

## What to measure (after 2-4 weeks, Search Console)
- Queries with impressions but low clicks -> improve title/description.
- Pages stuck at position 8-20 -> add depth, FAQ and internal links.
- Queries we did not plan for -> new articles.
